1. Stack Kya Hai?
 (The LIFO Principle)Defination: Stack ek aisi linear data structure hai jo LIFO (Last In, First Out) ke usool par kaam karti hai.Real-life Example: Socho plates ka dher (stack of plates). Jo plate tum sabse aakhir mein (upar) rakhoge, jab uthane ki baari aayegi toh wahi sabse pehle uthegi. Jo plate sabse neeche hogi, woh sabse aakhir mein bahar aayegi.Core Operations ($O(1)$ Time):push(item): Stack ke sabse upar (top par) naya item daalna.pop(): Stack ke sabse upar wale item ko remove karke wapas dena.peek() / top(): Sirf yeh dekhna ke abhi stack ke top par kaun sa item rakha hai (bina remove kiye).isEmpty(): Yeh check karna ke stack khali hai ya nahi.
2. Queue Kya Hai?
 (The FIFO Principle)Defination: Queue ek aisi linear data structure hai jo FIFO (First In, First Out) ke usool par kaam karti hai.Real-life Example: Bank ya ticket counter ki line. Jo banda line mein sabse pehle aakar khada hoga, ticket sabse pehle usi ko milegi. Jo aakhir mein aayega, usko sabse aakhir mein milegi.Core Operations ($O(1)$ Time):enqueue(item) / push(): Queue ke peechhe (rear/tail) par naya item jorna.dequeue(item) / pop(): Queue ke aage (front/head) se purana item nikalna.front(): Line mein sabse aage kaun khada hai, yeh dekhna.
