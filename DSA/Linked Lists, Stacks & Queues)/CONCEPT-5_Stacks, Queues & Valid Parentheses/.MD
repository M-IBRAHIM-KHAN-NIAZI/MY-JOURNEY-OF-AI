1. Stack Kya Hai?
 (The LIFO Principle)Defination: Stack ek aisi linear data structure hai jo LIFO (Last In, First Out) ke usool par kaam karti hai.Real-life Example: Socho plates ka dher (stack of plates). Jo plate tum sabse aakhir mein (upar) rakhoge, jab uthane ki baari aayegi toh wahi sabse pehle uthegi. Jo plate sabse neeche hogi, woh sabse aakhir mein bahar aayegi.
2. Queue Kya Hai?
 (The FIFO Principle)Defination: Queue ek aisi linear data structure hai jo FIFO (First In, First Out) ke usool par kaam karti hai.Real-life Example: Bank ya ticket counter ki line. Jo banda line mein sabse pehle aakar khada hoga, ticket sabse pehle usi ko milegi. Jo aakhir mein aayega, usko sabse aakhir mein milegi.

collections: Python's built-in module offering specialized container data types beyond standard lists, dicts, and tuples.

What deque is: Short for double-ended queue, allowing fast inserts and pops from both the front and back.

Why deque matters: A plain Python list shifts elements in O(n) time on pop(0) or insert(0), which slows down larger inputs.

Role & benefit: deque givesO(1) append, appendleft, pop, and popleft, making it ideal for queues, sliding windows, and BFS traversal.

Bottom line: Whenever you need fast FIFO queues or two-ended operations in Python, reach for collections.deque over a list.
