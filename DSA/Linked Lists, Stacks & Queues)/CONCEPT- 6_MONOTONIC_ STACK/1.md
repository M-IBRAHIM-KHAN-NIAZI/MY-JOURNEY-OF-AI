### Movement Logic Summary with Example (Daily Temperatures - LC 739) ###

**Example Input:** `temperatures = [73, 74, 75, 71, 69, 72, 76, 73]` $\rightarrow$ Output: `[1, 1, 4, 2, 1, 1, 0, 0]`.

**Step 0 (Setup & Initialization):**
* `n = len(temperatures)`: Total dinon ki count nikalta hai.
* `result = [0] * n`: Ek list banata hai jisme har din ke slot mein shuru se `0` rakha hota hai—agar aage koi zyada temperature wala din na mile, toh woh slot `0` hi rehta hai.
* `stack = []`: Ek khali list banati hai jo **din ke indices (`0, 1, 2...`)** store karti hai (values nahi), un dinon ke liye jo abhi aage aane wale kisi garam din ka wait kar rahe hain.

**Step 1 (Iterating Through Days - For Loop):**
* `for i in range(n):`: Left se right har current din `i` ko ek-ek karke uthata hai.
* Shuru mein (`i = 0`) `stack` khali hota hai, isliye `while` condition false rehti hai aur `0` seedha stack mein chala jata hai.

**Step 2 (Checking & Popping Warmer Matches - While Loop):**
* `while stack and temperatures[i] > temperatures[stack[-1]]:`:
  - `stack` check karta hai ke peeche koi din wait kar raha hai ya nahi.
  - `temperatures[i] > temperatures[stack[-1]]` check karta hai ke kya aaj ka temperature `i`, stack ke top (`stack[-1]`) wale purane din ke temperature se zyada hai.
  - Agar haan, toh monotonic decreasing order todhne wale purane din ko `prev_day = stack.pop()` karke bahar nikala jata hai.
  - Phir `result[prev_day] = i - prev_day` se us nikle hue purane din ke apne slot mein dinon ka farq (`i - prev_day`) likh diya jata hai.
  - Yeh `while` loop tab tak chalta hai jab tak stack khali na ho ya stack ke top ka temperature aaj se zyada/barabar na ho jaye. *(Example: `i = 5` par `72` aata hai toh pehle `4` (`69`) pop hota hai $\rightarrow$ `result[4] = 1`, phir `3` (`71`) pop hota hai $\rightarrow$ `result[3] = 2`).*

**Step 3 (Pushing Current Day & Final Return):**
* `stack.append(i)`: Jab peeche ke saare match hone wale din pop ho jayein (ya koi pop na ho), toh current din `i` ka index stack mein push kar diya jata hai taaki woh bhi aage ke liye wait kare aur stack ka decreasing order barqarar rahe.
* Loop khatam hone ke baad `return result` kar diya jata hai; jo indices stack mein bache reh jaate hain (jaise aakhir mein `76` aur `73`), unke slots mein pehle se rakha `0` hi rehta hai.
