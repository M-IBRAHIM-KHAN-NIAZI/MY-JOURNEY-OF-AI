1. Simple Practical Example (Conceptual)
Real-Life Scenario: Soch ek gol running track (circular track) hai.

Tune do logon ko daudne ke liye chhoda: ek Slow runner (jo 1 second mein 1 kadam chalta hai) aur ek Fast runner (jo 1 second mein 2 kadam chalta hai).

Case A (Agar rasta gol/circular hai): Fast runner aage nikal jayega, lekin gol ghoom kar wapas peeche aayega aur ek point par slow runner ko overtake karke dobara touch kar lega. Iska matlab hai track mein cycle (loop) mojood hai!

Case B (Agar rasta seedha/linear hai): Fast runner seedha bhaag kar aage khatam raste (None) par nikal jayega aur kabhi wapas nahi aayega. Iska matlab koi cycle nahi hai.


WHAT IS NODE?
Programming aur Linked Lists ki duniya mein "Node" ek chota sa container ya dabba hota hai jo data ko store karta hai aur aage wale node ka rasta (address) yaad rakhta hai.

Jub hum normal arrays (jaise [1, 2, 3]) ki baat karte hain, toh saare elements memory mein ek sath line se jude hote hain. Lekin Linked List mein elements alag-alag jagah par pade hote hain, aur unhe aapas mein jodne ke liye har element ko ek Node ki shakal di jati hai.
